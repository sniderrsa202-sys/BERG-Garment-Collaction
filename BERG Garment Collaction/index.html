BERG / FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

How do I place an order?

Choose your item, select a size and colour, then add it to your cart. Once you are happy with the selection, choose your delivery location and complete checkout via WhatsApp.

How do I order through WhatsApp?

Use the WhatsApp link in the site or the product details panel, then send the selected product name, price, size and colour. BERG will confirm the order and payment arrangements directly.

What payment methods are available?

Payment arrangements are confirmed directly with BERG before the order is finalised. Do not send payment details through an unofficial or public channel.

What delivery options and delivery times are available?

Delivery is calculated in the cart based on your selected location: Gauteng — R60 and Outside Gauteng — R150. No exact delivery time is promised unless BERG confirms it separately.

What is your returns and exchanges policy?

Returns and exchanges are handled according to BERG’s returns information. Please review the returns page or contact BERG directly for item-specific guidance.

How do I choose the right clothing size?

If you are unsure about sizing, contact BERG on WhatsApp or phone before placing the order to confirm the best fit. BERG does not currently publish a confirmed size guide on this site.
