BERG / PRIVACY

Privacy
Policy

This page explains how information may be handled when you contact BERG, place an order, or request delivery.

Personal information

Personal information may include your name, contact details, order details, and information you choose to provide to BERG.

Contact information

BERG may use your phone number and WhatsApp contact details to respond to questions, confirm orders, and provide customer support.

Delivery information

Delivery information may include your address, location, access notes, and other details needed to arrange delivery.

Orders

Information supplied with an order may be used to identify the products requested, calculate the total, arrange delivery, and communicate about the order.

WhatsApp communication

WhatsApp checkout opens an external WhatsApp conversation with the order information you choose to send. WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices also apply.

Customer support

BERG may use information from support requests to investigate delivery, product, return, exchange, or website questions.

When information may be shared

Information may be shared with a courier or other service provider only when reasonably needed to process delivery, support, or an order-related request.

Customer privacy

BERG should handle customer information carefully and avoid using it for unrelated purposes. Customers should avoid sending sensitive information through public or unofficial channels.

Your questions

To ask about personal information you have provided, contact BERG on 081 842 5151 or WhatsApp.
