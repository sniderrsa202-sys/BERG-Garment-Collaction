We use the delivery location selected in the cart to calculate the delivery fee for your order.

Delivery fees

Gauteng: R60
Outside Gauteng: R150

Delivery locations

Select Gauteng or Outside Gauteng in the cart before checkout. The selected fee is added once to the order total.

Customer address requirements

Provide a complete, accurate address, including suburb, city, postal code, access instructions, and a working contact number where needed.

Delivery processing

Orders are prepared after order details and payment arrangements are confirmed. BERG will use the contact details supplied with the order.

Courier or service delivery

Orders may be sent through a courier or delivery service selected by BERG. Delivery arrangements can vary by location and service availability.

Possible delays

Delivery can be delayed by courier capacity, weather, public holidays, incomplete information, or circumstances outside BERG's control. No exact delivery time is promised unless separately confirmed.

If nobody is available

The courier may contact you, leave a delivery notice, or arrange another attempt. Any additional arrangement should be confirmed with the courier or BERG.

Incorrect address information

Customers are responsible for checking their address. Incorrect or incomplete information may delay delivery or lead to additional delivery arrangements.

Delivery problems

Contact BERG as soon as possible if your order is delayed, missing, or delivered to the wrong place. Have your order and contact details ready.
